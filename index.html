02. projects

Poisson-boundaries entropy and ultralimits

arxiv journal follow-up

My master thesis under the supervision of Yehuda Shalom - on establishing a ultralimit construction for amenable group actions via Poisson boundaries with applications to minimizing entropy on such actions. It resulted in two papers: "Entropy, ultralimits and the Poisson boundary", and a follow-up, "Ultralimits, Amenable actions and Entropy".
